{% trans "Chat privacy note" %}

{% blocktrans %}Chats on Mtaani Connect are private between the two members in the conversation. This page states plainly what V1 protects — and what it does not.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Messages travel over TLS and are stored on our servers. There is NO end-to-end encryption in this version; it is being evaluated for a later release. We say that openly instead of implying more protection than there is.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}The system never shows or posts your phone number. A phone number reaches a chat only when a member types it in themselves — that is an explicit choice, made per message.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}When a message looks like it carries a phone number, moderation gets a flag for scam-pattern review (phone-number scams are the top marketplace risk). A flag never blocks your message by itself.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}A reported message goes to human review in the moderation queue.{% endblocktrans %}
